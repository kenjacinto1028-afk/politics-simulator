# Mayor design (v2)

Goal: a first race you usually win if you play actively (~75%), and a Mayor job with its own decisions.

## Voter groups
Workers 35%, Business 20%, Retirees 25%, Students 20% of the electorate. Each has an approval of you (0–100).
- In office, each group drifts toward what it cares about: workers → jobs + welfare; business → low tax + jobs; retirees → low crime + services; students → culture + services. Laws shift these targets.
- Elections: the weighted group approval adds to your score (±10 at the extremes).
- Action **Meet a group**: +3–6 with that group.

## City council (7 seats)
Each member leans toward one group and has a relationship with you (0–100). In one-party states it is the party committee; in corporate states, the board.
- A proposal passes with 4+ yes votes. A member votes yes when relationship + 4 × (how much the proposal helps their group) ± noise ≥ 50. The UI shows a forecast per member.
- **Lobby** a member: +8–14 relationship (1 action). **Cut a deal**: $20k funds, +20 relationship.
- Relationships drift toward your popularity.

## Projects (up to 2 under construction)
Cost comes from the city treasury when the council approves. On completion: a ribbon-cutting (+popularity), group bonuses, and a lasting boost to city stats.

## Laws
Need council approval. While active they shift city-stat targets, group targets and sometimes revenue. Repeal any time (small popularity hit).

## Next office
After 18 months as Mayor with popularity ≥ 55, **Run for higher office** starts a 6-month campaign for the next rung. Lose and you stay Mayor. Win and you move up. Gameplay for higher offices comes in the next update.

## Tips
Each month the campaign panel shows one tip based on your weakest spot (a group, funds, debates, standing).
